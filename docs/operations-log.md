# Operations log

Every failure and fix from the build, with before and after numbers where a number exists. This log becomes part 4 of the case-study wrapper ("what broke, how you found it, what you changed"). In interviews, the failure log outperforms the demo.

## How to use it

Log the friction at the moment it happens, not retroactively. When a step runs long, stop and log why. The entry is the finding.
